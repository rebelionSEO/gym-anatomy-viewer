About this demo

This is placeholder demo hardware. The 3D model is primitive boxes standing in for muscles; the activation curves are heuristic approximations, not EMG measurements. Nothing here is anatomically accurate.

Replace human_anatomy_gym_v1.glb with a real rigged anatomy glb that uses the muscles/{name} node convention from ui_manifest.json and the viewer continues working. For meshes with any other naming, drag & drop a .glb, then use Edit mapping… to map each muscle key to a mesh in your glb. Mappings are saved per filename in your browser.

If a mapped mesh has morph targets named contract_{muscle}_01 they are driven directly from the activation values. Otherwise the viewer falls back to tint + a small scale pulse.
